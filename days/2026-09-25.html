01 occupy /ˈɑːkjupaɪ/ v.
词义
占用；占据（职位/空间）；使忙碌
词根
oc-（向）+ cupy（抓取）→ 抓住并占有
词源
拉丁 occupare「占据、占有」
考点
办公/地产：occupy a position；the office occupies two floors
搭配
occupy a positionoccupy spacefully occupied
例句
She will occupy the regional manager position starting next month. 她将从下个月起担任区域经理一职。

搭配
